## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address and, if you add one, a display name. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).
- **Location:** the postal code you enter. It is stored on your device. If you save it to your profile, we store it with your account. We use it only to find nearby stores.
- **Price reports:** the store, item, price, date and (optionally) whether it was a sale.
- **Things you save:** shopping trips you log and price alerts you create.
- **Technical data:** error reports (without your name or email) and basic server logs, such as IP address and time of request, kept for security and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising trackers.

## How we use it

- To run the service: compare prices, plan trips, and show your saved data.
- To show **shared store prices**. Price reports are combined with other shoppers' reports and shown without your name.
- To keep the service safe: rate limits, spotting fake or abusive reports.
- To email you about new features **only if you opt in**. You can opt out at any time.

## How long we keep it

- Account data is kept while your account is open.
- Price reports are used for current prices for 30 days. After that they are kept for history, without any link to you once you delete your account.
- Server logs are kept for [30] days.

## Your rights

- **Access and download:** Account → Download my data.
- **Correct:** edit your profile at any time.
- **Delete:** Account → Delete my account. This removes your account, saved trips and alerts. Your past price reports stay, but are de-identified so they can't be linked to you.
- **Withdraw consent** to optional emails at any time.
- **Complain:** contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.
