# About our prices

SmartCart never makes up prices. Here is where every number comes from.

## Store prices

- Shoppers report prices they paid or saw on the shelf, with the date.
- For each store and item, we show the **middle value (median)** of reports from the **last 30 days**, with how many reports it's based on and how recent the latest one is.
- Reports far from the usual price are checked before they're shown.
- If there are no recent reports, we show that we don't know the price rather than guessing.

## Flyer deals

Flyer prices come from published weekly flyers and are only shown while the flyer is valid.

## Trip costs

Fuel cost uses an average regular gas price for Edmonton and a typical fuel use of about 9.5 L per 100 km. Distances are straight-line estimates, so real driving distance will be a bit longer.

## Always check the shelf

Prices change often, and stores may price differently from day to day. The price at the store is the one that counts.
