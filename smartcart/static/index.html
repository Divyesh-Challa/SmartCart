When do weekly supermarket circulars refresh?

In Canada, grocery flyers officially launch every Thursday morning. SmartCart syncs circulars, sale items, and recipe costs automatically at 12:01 AM every Thursday.

How does the 3-Plan Route Optimizer work?

It evaluates all grocery combinations across competing supermarkets within your radius. Plan 1 finds the best multi-store split only if the grocery savings exceed the extra fuel and travel distance.
