The Developer Behind SmartCart

Why I built SmartCart.

DC
Divyesh Challa
Independent Canadian Developer in Edmonton, AB
Student Developer No Ads Open Source

SmartCart started with watching my mom flip through paper supermarket flyers at the kitchen table every Thursday evening, circling bargains with a red pen and planning out which stores had the best prices.

As food inflation surged across Canada over the last few years, picking up basic groceries quickly turned into a $100 plus receipt for barely two bags. Comparing prices between stores became essential, but it created an entirely new problem: driving to multiple stores across Edmonton only makes sense if you do not burn more in vehicle fuel than you actually save on groceries.

I wanted one simple, transparent tool that reads weekly flyers, creates delicious dinner recipes from what is discounted, and computes whether splitting your shopping trip across two stores actually saves you money after factoring in live gasoline prices and driving distance.

SmartCart is built independently right here in Edmonton. It is free to use, contains no advertisements, and sells zero personal data.
